# Phase 2.7 — Suggestions from Photos Implementation Plan

**Roadmap:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-completion-roadmap.md` → Milestone 2 → 2.7 (design in §3.6)

**Goal:** Werejugo notices what the photos say and offers to fill in the gaps, one tap each.
- **A trip:**
  - "143 photos taken during Italy 2024. Add them?"
  - "You took 23 photos near Florence on 5 June. Add it as a place?"
  - "Grandma is in 12 photos from this trip. Add her?"
- **A place:** "12 photos from here. Add them?"
- **The library:** "Looks like you were in Lisbon, 3–9 March 2019 (143 photos). Create a trip?"

Each suggestion can be dismissed.

**Decisions this plan relies on:**
- **§2 (2026-09-27):** people, location and date context drive suggestions, for current trips and items and for new trips and items (owner's answer).
- **2.4:** the picker already ranks "then and there" photos for any item. A suggestion's **Review** opens that picker, so there's one place to choose photos.
- **2.5:** face tags are links, so "who's in these photos" is a join.

---

### Constraints found while planning

- **Place names come from Immich.** Immich reverse-geocodes every photo with a location (city, state, country, from GeoNames data it ships with). The sync keeps them on `media` (`city`, `state`, `country`); existing photos get them at the next nightly full sync. Werejugo calls no geocoding service.
- **"You were away" needs a home.** No family has a home location, so it's inferred: the area (a 0.5° grid cell) where photos were taken on the most distinct days. Photos more than 50 km from it are "away".
  - A small library without a clear home (fewer than 5 days in any one area) treats everywhere as away.
  - This is logged as §7 Q9: a home set in Settings could come later, and is the alternative.
- **What counts as a trip found in photos:**
  - Photos that are away, in no trip, and not taken within an existing trip's dates (±1 day). Photos taken during an existing trip are that trip's suggestion instead.
  - Taken one after another with no gap longer than 2 days, and at least 6 of them.
  - Named after the most common city ("Lisbon", or "Lisbon and Porto" when a second city has a quarter of the photos), else the country. Photos without a location taken during it are counted too.
- **Suggestions are computed when asked,** not stored. They're one or two indexed queries per item. The library's trips read the family's geotagged photos that are in no trip (20,000 rows in about 100 ms), so no background job or cache is needed. Playwright measures it at 20,000 photos.
- **Dismissing:** `suggestion_dismissals (family_id, key, dismissed_at)`.
  - A photo suggestion (`trip-photos:…`, `visit-photos:…`) comes back only for photos added after it was dismissed.
  - A place, person or new-trip suggestion stays dismissed.
- **Keys** name a suggestion stably, so applying and dismissing can re-check it on the server:
  - `trip-photos:<trip>`, `trip-place:<trip>:<lat>,<lng>` (3 decimals), `trip-person:<trip>:<person>`, `visit-photos:<visit>`;
  - `new-trip:<first day>:<last day>:<lat>,<lng>` (1 decimal).

  Applying recomputes the suggestion; one that's gone is a 404 "This suggestion has changed".

### Data model (migration `0010_photo_suggestions.sql`)

- `media.city`, `media.state`, `media.country` (TEXT, from Immich's reverse geocoding).
- `suggestion_dismissals (family_id → families, key TEXT, dismissed_at, PRIMARY KEY (family_id, key))`.
- An index for "my photos in no trip": `media (family_id, taken_at) WHERE trip_id IS NULL AND hidden_at IS NULL`.

### The suggestions (`lib/suggest.ts`)

| For | Suggests | Applying |
|---|---|---|
| A trip I'm on | **Photos:** mine, in no trip, not hidden, taken during its dates ±1 day; near one of its places (50 km) when it has places, or with no location. | Puts them in the trip (the album follows, 2.6). |
| | **Places:** my photos in the trip clustered by location (400 m, 3 photos or more), more than 1 km from every place on the trip. Labelled "Near *city*", with the date and count, most photos first, at most 8. | Creates the place on the trip (my family's), dated by its first photo, and links its photos. |
| | **People:** people in my photos of the trip (tagged by face or by hand, 2 photos or more), not yet linked to the trip. | Links the person to the trip. |
| A place I can see | **Photos:** mine, not linked to it, within 2 km, taken on its dates ±1 day; not in another trip. | Links them to the place (and to its trip, as `createLink` does). |
| My library | **Trips:** found as above, newest first, at most 20, each with the photos' place names, dates, count and 4 thumbnails. | Creates the trip (my family's, status *done*, named "*Lisbon*, March 2019" unless another name is given) and puts the photos in it. Its places then show as place suggestions. |

### API (`routes/suggestions.ts`)

| Route | What it does |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/suggestions?for=trip:<id>\|visit:<id>\|library` | The suggestions: `{ items: [{ key, kind, title, detail, count, thumbUrls, … }] }`. A trip or place I can't see is a 404. |
| `POST /api/suggestions/apply` | `{ key, name? }` → what was made or changed: `{ tripId? , visitId?, attached? }`. |
| `POST /api/suggestions/dismiss` | `{ key }` → 204. |

- **Isolation cases:** another family's trip, place or person in a key is a 404; library suggestions only ever show my photos.

### Frontend

- **`SuggestionCard`** (kit style): a title ("143 photos taken during this trip"), a short detail, up to 4 thumbnails, and buttons: the main action ("Add them", "Add place", "Add Grandma", "Create trip"), **Review** where it's photos (opens `PhotoPicker` on the item), and ✕ to dismiss. Applying shows a toast and refreshes the item.
- **A trip:** a "Suggestions" block under Photos, with photos first, then places, then people.
- **A place:** under its photos.
- **Planning:** "Trips in your photos" above the trip list. The name can be edited before **Create trip**, and then the new trip opens.
- **Photos page:** when there are trips in the photos, a one-line banner: "Your photos suggest 3 trips → Planning".

### Tests (TDD, in order)

1. **Migration:** the columns and the table. The sync fills `city`/`country` from Immich's EXIF, and the stand-in sends them.
2. **Contract (real Immich):** a photo with GPS near Paris comes back with `city` "Paris" once Immich has read it.
3. **`lib/suggest.ts`** (SQL fixtures, no Immich):
   - **Trip photos:** the dates and places rules, hidden and other-trip photos left out, and dismissal letting only newer photos back in.
   - **Places:** clustering, near existing places left out, labels.
   - **People:** face tags and hand tags counted, already-linked people left out.
   - **Place photos:** distance and date.
   - **Library trips:** home inference, splitting on 2-day gaps, at least 6 photos, existing trips' dates left out, naming with one or two cities or a country, and no home in a small library.
4. **Routes:**
   - Apply for each kind: the trip made with its photos; the place made and linked; the person linked; photos attached, with the album kicked.
   - A changed suggestion is a 404; dismissing; isolation cases.
5. **Frontend units:** `SuggestionCard` actions; the trip's, place's and Planning's suggestion lists (apply, review, dismiss); the Photos banner.
6. **Playwright** (stand-in, nginx):
   - A seeded library: home near Tahoe, a week in Lisbon, a weekend in Porto, photos during "Italy 2024" near its places and one near Florence with no place, and Grandma's face in some.
   - Planning suggests Lisbon and Porto, and Create trip makes one.
   - The trip suggests its photos, the Florence place and Grandma. Apply each.
   - A place suggests its photos.
   - Dismissing.
   - With 20,000 photos, the library's suggestions arrive within 1 s.
   - Phone and desktop.
