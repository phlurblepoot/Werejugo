# Phase 1.5 — Shared Trips & Cross-Family People Implementation Plan

**Roadmap:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-completion-roadmap.md` → Milestone 1 → 1.5 (design sketch in §3.3–§3.4)
**Goal:** Two families can plan and remember a trip together. The host invites another family with a one-time link and picks its role; both add places, itinerary and photos; each family's packing, documents and bookings stay private; everything shows who added it; a family that leaves takes its contributions with it. "Grandma" can be one person across both families.

**Decisions this plan relies on:** roadmap §2 (trips shared between families; host picks the role per invite — co-owner or contributor; one-time invite links; shared-trip photos only inside the trip; packing, bookings/documents and budget private per family; people linkable across families) and the §7 defaults (a leaving family's contributions leave with it and come back if re-invited; family owners accept trip invites).

---

### Data model (migration `0002_shared_trips.sql`)

- `trip_members(trip_id, family_id, role 'coowner'|'contributor', invited_by, joined_at)` — **guest** families only; the host is `trips.family_id` (so every existing trip is simply "hosted, not shared").
- `trip_invites(id, trip_id, role, token_hash, note, created_by, created_at, expires_at, used_at, used_by_family, revoked_at)` — one-time, hashed like account invites.
- `person_links(id, person_a, person_b, status 'pending'|'accepted', requested_by, requested_family, created_at, responded_at)` — one pair once (unique on the unordered pair).
- `activity(id bigserial, trip_id, family_id, user_id, kind, target_type, target_id, summary, at)` — who added what to which trip.
- Content rows keep their **author** family (`family_id`) and user (`created_by`); nothing moves when a trip is shared.

### Access rules (all in `lib/access.ts`)

| Kind | Readable by my family when… | Editable when… |
|---|---|---|
| trip | mine, or I'm a member | mine (host): details, status, share links, members, delete |
| visit, itinerary item | mine; or on a trip I can see **and** its author family is still on that trip | mine; or I'm host/co-owner of that trip (same "still on the trip" rule) |
| media | mine; or on a trip I can see and its family is still on it — **only inside that trip** (trip filter / by id), never in my library list | mine only |
| comment | its place is readable | its author (as today) |
| person | mine; or its family shares a trip with mine (name + avatar only) | mine only |
| link | both ends are readable by me | its creating family |
| documents, packing, blackouts, themes, icons, map sets, share links | mine only (private, even on a shared trip) | mine only |

- `assertRefs` gains modes: references a family may *use* (e.g. put a place on a shared trip, tag another family's person on a shared place) vs rows it must *own* (avatars, photos it re-files).
- Lists: `GET /api/trips` returns shared trips with `role`, host family and member count; `GET /api/visits` includes visible shared-trip places with attribution; `GET /api/media?trip=<shared trip>` includes the other families' photos; the library view stays mine only.
- The tenant-isolation suite keeps passing (no sharing ⇒ nothing visible) and gains a shared-trip suite: member vs. non-member, contributor vs. co-owner, private kinds never cross, a family that leaves disappears and comes back on re-invite.

### API

- Trip sharing: `GET /api/trips/:id/members` (families + roles; pending invites for the host), `POST /api/trips/:id/invites {role, note?, expiresInDays}` (host), `DELETE /api/trips/:id/invites/:inviteId`, `GET /api/trip-invites/:token` (signed-in preview), `POST /api/trip-invites/:token/accept` (an owner of a family not already on the trip), `PATCH /api/trips/:id/members/:familyId {role}` (host), `DELETE /api/trips/:id/members/:familyId` (host removes, or the member family's owner leaves).
- Activity: `GET /api/trips/:id/activity`; events recorded for places, itinerary items, photos and comments added to a trip, and members joining, leaving, being removed or changing role.
- People: `GET /api/trips/:id/people` (everyone's people on the trip, for tagging and linking), `GET/POST /api/person-links`, `POST /api/person-links/:id/accept`, `DELETE /api/person-links/:id`; `GET /api/people/:id` lists linked people; `GET /api/relations?entity=person:…` merges a linked person's relations that the viewer may see.
- Attribution: shared-trip DTOs carry `familyId`/`familyName` (and `createdByName` where it exists).

### Frontend

- Planning: shared trips on the board and timeline with a "Shared" chip (host family for guests); the trip view gets a **Families** section (host: invite with role + expiry → one-time link, change role, remove; guests: leave), **attribution chips** on itinerary items, role-aware controls (only the host edits trip details/status/sharing; contributors change only their own items), an **Activity** list, and "only your family sees this" on Bookings and Packing.
- `/trip-invite/:token`: preview, then "Join this trip" for family owners (members are told to ask their owner); signing in first returns to the invite.
- Photos: a shared trip's album shows every family's photos with a "by <family>" chip; other families' photos are view-only.
- People: "Same person in another family" on a person (link to someone from a family you share a trip with), incoming link requests to accept or decline, linked people's shared-trip relations shown on the person.
- Dev seed: a second family ("The Smiths") and a shared trip with contributions from both, so the feature is visible on a fresh dev database.

### Tests

Backend: invite/accept (owner only, one use, expiry, not twice, not the host), roles (contributor edits own only; co-owner edits others'; host-only trip details, sharing and members), leave/remove hides contributions and re-invite restores them, private kinds never cross, photo library vs. trip album, person links (propose only with a shared trip, accept by the other family, merged relations), activity events, and the tenant-isolation suite unchanged. Frontend: invite accept page, trip families section, attribution chips, role-aware controls, link requests.
