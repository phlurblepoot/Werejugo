# Phase 2.5 — Faces → People Implementation Plan

**Roadmap:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-completion-roadmap.md` → Milestone 2 → 2.5 (design in §3.6)

**Goal:** Immich recognises faces; Werejugo turns them into its people.
- Each family sees the faces Immich found in its photos and says who each one is: someone already in Werejugo (including a person from another family, like a shared grandparent), a new person, or nobody to keep.
- From then on, every photo with that face is tagged with that person, and new photos are tagged as they arrive.
- New faces wait in a review queue.

**Decisions this plan relies on:**
- **§2 (2026-09-27):** people found in photos drive suggestions (owner's answer). The tags made here are what 2.7 builds on.
- **§2 (2026-09-27):** stock Immich; its cluster groups can share face recognition between accounts.
- **Person links (1.5):** "our Grandma is your Grandma" already shows each family's photos of her on both families' pages.

---

### Constraints found while planning

- **Immich's people are per account.** Each family's Immich account has its own people, even for the same grandparent.
  - Immich 3's **cluster groups** let accounts share face clustering, but the API doesn't say which of another account's people is the same person. There's nothing for Werejugo to read.
  - Cross-family works through Werejugo instead: a family can map its face to another family's person (one it can see, on a shared trip or through a person link). Person links already carry photos across.
  - Cluster groups can come later, once they can be tested (see the next point); this is logged.
- **The CI Immich runs no machine learning,** so it recognises no faces. The contract test instead makes people and faces by hand (`createPerson`, then `createFace` on an uploaded photo), which is what recognition produces. Everything Werejugo reads — people, their thumbnails, assets by person — is exercised that way.
- **Recognition runs after upload** and doesn't necessarily change an asset's `updatedAt`, so watching changed assets isn't enough. Face tags are reconciled **per mapped person** (search assets by that person): nightly, whenever a mapping changes, and for people Immich updated since the last sync.
- **Tags are ordinary links** (media ↔ person) with role `face`, so filters, relations, person pages and suggestions pick them up unchanged. Only `face` links are ever removed by this, never tags someone added by hand.

### Data model (migration `0008_faces.sql`)

- **`immich_people`:** one row per Immich person in a family's account.
  - `id`, `family_id`, `immich_person_id` (unique per family), `name`, `birth_date`, `hidden_in_immich`.
  - `photo_count`; `person_id` (the Werejugo person, `ON DELETE SET NULL`); `ignored`.
  - `immich_updated_at`, `first_seen_at`, `reconciled_at`.
- Links with role `face` are the tags; a partial index on `links (to_id) WHERE role = 'face'`.

### Adapter and contract additions

- `immich.listPeople(conn)`: all pages, hidden included.
- `immich.personStats(conn, id)`: photo count.
- `immich.renamePerson(conn, id, name)`.
- `fetchPersonThumbnail(conn, id)`.
- Asset search by person: `filter.personIds.any`.
- **Contract tests** (stand-in and real Immich):
  - Create a person and a face on an uploaded photo.
  - The person is listed; the search by person finds the photo; the statistics count it; renaming shows; the thumbnail is served (after Immich makes it).
- **Stand-in:** people, faces, person search, statistics and thumbnails.

### Sync (`lib/immich/faces.ts`, pg-boss `immich-faces` per family)

1. Refresh `immich_people` from `listPeople`: new ones are added as "new"; names and hidden flags are updated; people gone from Immich are deleted (their `face` links go too).
2. For people without a count, or that Immich changed, read the count (at most 200 a run).
3. For each **mapped** person that's new, changed, or on the nightly run: search that person's assets and reconcile their `face` links with the family's photos. Photos Werejugo doesn't have yet are tagged when they arrive (the upload hand-off and library sync look up face tags for new assets).

**When it runs:**
- After each library sync (so every 5 minutes, and nightly in full).
- Straight away when a mapping changes.
- On **Refresh from Immich**.

### API (`routes/faces.ts`, family-scoped)

| Route | What it does |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/faces?view=review\|mapped\|ignored\|all` | Faces with a signed thumbnail link, their name in Immich, photo count, and the person they're mapped to (name, family). **Review** is new, not hidden in Immich, with 2 photos or more, most photos first. |
| `GET /api/faces/count` | How many are waiting for review, for the badge. |
| `PATCH /api/faces/:id` | `{ personId }` to map, `null` to unmap, or `{ ignored: true\|false }`. The person must be one the family can use (`assertRefs`), which includes another family's person it can see. Mapping reconciles the tags at once and returns how many photos were tagged. If the face has no name in Immich, it's given the person's name there too. |
| `POST /api/faces/:id/person` | `{ displayName }`: create a Werejugo person from the face and map it. |
| `GET /api/f/:id` | The face thumbnail, at a signed link (like `/api/m`). |

- **Isolation cases** for every route.

### Frontend

- **People page:** a **Faces** view beside the list, with a count badge while faces wait for review. It has the tabs To review / Matched / Ignored and a grid of face thumbnails with name and photo count.
- **Tapping a face:** "Who is this?" offers a search over people (mine first, then other families' people I can see, each marked with their family), **New person** (with the Immich name filled in), and **Not someone we keep**.
  - Mapping says "Tagged in 124 photos".
  - A matched face can be changed or unmatched.
- **Rail badge:** People shows the review count, like Documents' due count.
- **A person's page:** "Found in photos: 2 faces", with their thumbnails. The photos come in through the existing related list, as before.
- **Photos page:** the person filter now finds face-tagged photos too; it already uses links.

### Tests (TDD, in order)

1. **Migration:** the table, the constraints, and cascading when a person or family is deleted.
2. **Faces sync (stand-in):**
   - New people are added; renames and hiding are followed; a person gone from Immich loses their row and tags.
   - Mapping tags every photo with that face, and only those; unmapping removes `face` links only; hand tags are kept.
   - A new photo with a mapped face is tagged at the next sync.
   - Counts are read.
3. **Routes:**
   - Review ordering and threshold.
   - Mapping to my person, and to another family's linked person.
   - Refusing a person I can't see.
   - Creating a person from a face.
   - Ignoring, and the counts.
   - The thumbnail proxy (signature, other family).
   - Isolation.
4. **Frontend units:** the Faces grid, the "Who is this?" picker (search, new person, ignore), and the badge.
5. **Playwright** (stand-in with faces made through its API):
   - Review three faces: one to an existing person, one to a new person, one ignored.
   - The person's page shows the tagged photos, and the photo filter by person finds them.
   - A face mapped to the other family's linked Grandma shows her photos on both families' pages.
   - Phone and desktop.
