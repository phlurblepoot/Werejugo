# Phase 2.4 — Library UI Implementation Plan

**Roadmap:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-completion-roadmap.md` → Milestone 2 → 2.4 (design in §3.6)

**Goal:** a family's whole library — 20,000 photos and videos or more — browses as smoothly as Immich's own timeline.
- Jump to any year.
- Filter by person, trip, place, date, and photos or videos.
- Open anything full-screen, with video, and step through with the arrow keys or a swipe.
- Hide what doesn't belong (receipts, screenshots) without deleting it from Immich.
- Act on many photos at once.
- The Photos map shows every geotagged photo, clustered, with thumbnails.
- Every place, trip, person and itinerary item gets **Add photos**, a picker over the whole library. It opens on the photos taken then and there.

**Decisions this plan relies on:**
- **§2 (2026-09-27):** the photo picker browses the whole library, starting from the item's date and place (owner's answer).
- **§3.6:** the metadata cache (date, place, kind, size, `hidden`) is what makes filters, the map and joins fast.

---

### Constraints found while planning

- **Today's grid can't scale.** It fetches pages of 60 and renders every photo it has loaded, so 20,000 photos means 20,000 DOM nodes. The map only knows the loaded pages (noted in 2.2).
- **Windowing:** the timeline is laid out from **per-month counts** before any photos load, so the scrollbar is right from the start and a jump to 2014 lands on 2014. Only the months on screen (plus a margin) are fetched and rendered. Rows are fixed height, so the layout is arithmetic, not measurement. `@tanstack/react-virtual` does the windowing (same family as React Query, about 5 KB).
- **Map thumbnails:** signing a link for 20,000 thumbnails up front would be several MB. The map gets compact points (id, lng, lat), and signed thumbnail links only for the clusters on screen, 60 at most.
- **Hidden** is Werejugo's own flag. The photo stays in Immich untouched, and the sync never clears the flag.
- **Itinerary items aren't link targets.** Photos picked for one go to its trip, and to its place too once it has been turned into one.

### Data model (migration `0007_media_library.sql`)

- `media.hidden_at TIMESTAMPTZ` (NULL = shown).
- An index for the timeline: `(family_id, (COALESCE(taken_at, created_at)) DESC, id DESC)`, partial on `hidden_at IS NULL`.

### API

| Route | What it does |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/media/timeline?filters` | `{ months: [{ month: "2025-07", count }], total }`, newest first. The same filters as the list. |
| `GET /api/media?month=2025-07&filters` | That month's photos, in the same order, with the cursor as today (limit up to 500). |
| `GET /api/media/geo?filters` | `{ points: [[id, lng, lat, kind]] }`: every geotagged photo that matches, unpaged. |
| `POST /api/media/links` | `{ ids }` (at most 200) → `{ [id]: { thumbUrl, url } }`, for the ones the caller may read. |
| `PATCH /api/media/:id` | Also takes `hidden: boolean`. |
| `POST /api/media/bulk` | `{ mediaIds, hidden?, tripId? }`: hide, show, or set the trip for many photos at once (own photos only). |
| `GET /api/media/for?entity=visit:…\|trip:…\|person:…\|itinerary:…` | The picker's opening suggestions and window (see below). |
| `POST /api/media/attach` | `{ mediaIds, to: "visit:…"\|"trip:…"\|"person:…"\|"itinerary:…" }`: link them all in one transaction. A trip sets `trip_id`, and a visit applies the trip inheritance `createLink` already does. |

- **New filters:** `kind=image|video`, `hidden=only`, `noTrip=1`. Hidden photos are left out of every list, count and map unless `hidden=only`. They still show where they're explicitly linked (a place's photos), since that was a choice.
- **`/api/media/for`:** each kind of item gives a date window and a place.
  - **Place:** the day of its visit ± 1 day; within 2 km of its point.
  - **Trip:** its dates ± 1 day; near any of its places.
  - **Person:** the trips they're linked to; their dates.
  - **Itinerary item:** its day; its point.

  It returns up to 300 suggested photos, those matching both the dates and the place first, then either. It also returns the window, so the picker can open the library at the right month. Photos already attached are marked.
- **Isolation cases** for every new route.

### Frontend

- **`LibraryTimeline`** (`components/photos/LibraryTimeline.tsx`), used by the Photos page and the picker.
  - **Layout:** from `timeline` + container width (cells about 150 px on desktop, 3 columns on phones) it builds rows: a month header, then photo rows.
  - **Loading and rendering:** it windows the rows with `@tanstack/react-virtual`, fetches each month on screen once through React Query, and shows neutral placeholders until it arrives.
  - **Year rail:** down the right side on desktop, a menu on phones; it jumps to any year.
  - **Select mode:** tick photos (click, Shift-click for a range, long-press on phones).
- **Photos page:**
  - The grid is `LibraryTimeline`.
  - Filters gain Photos/Videos, No trip and Hidden.
  - A selection bar with **Add to trip…**, **Remove from trip**, **Hide** / **Show**, and **Delete** (with a confirmation; it goes to Immich's trash).
- **Photo viewer** (replaces the `PhotoDetail` modal):
  - Full-screen, the image or video large, details beside it on desktop and below it on phones.
  - ←/→, swiping and on-screen arrows step through the timeline in order. It crosses month boundaries, loading the next month.
  - Esc closes. `/photos?photo=` still opens one.
  - Actions: caption, trip, people and places (the existing related panel), Download original, Hide, Delete.
- **Photos map:** it uses `/api/media/geo` with the page's filters. Clusters on screen show a thumbnail of one of their photos with the count; a single point shows its thumbnail. Clicking a cluster zooms; clicking a photo opens the viewer.
- **`PhotoPicker`** (a kit Modal, full-screen on phones), titled "Add photos to *Rome*":
  - First "Taken then and there" (the suggestions, all ticked by default when the item has none yet), then "Your whole library" (`LibraryTimeline` in select mode, opened at the window's month) with its filters.
  - **Add N photos** attaches through `/api/media/attach`. **Upload new** uses the upload manager with the same link.
  - Photos already attached show as such.
- **Entry points:**
  - A place's detail: **Add photos**, next to its photos.
  - A trip's detail: a new **Photos** section with the count, **Open album** and **Add photos**.
  - A person's page: **Add photos** instead of the text picker for photos.
  - Each itinerary row: **Add photos**.

### Tests (TDD, in order)

1. **Migration:** `hidden_at`, and the index is used for the timeline query (`EXPLAIN` in a test, with 2,000 rows).
2. **Routes:**
   - **Timeline:** months and counts for every filter; hidden left out.
   - **List:** a month's photos.
   - **Geo:** points only for geotagged, visible photos.
   - **Links:** only readable ids, and at most 200.
   - **Hiding:** hide and show, one or in bulk.
   - **Picker:** the window and ranking for a place, trip, person and itinerary item; attach for each kind, including trip inheritance and already-attached photos.
   - **Isolation cases.**
3. **Sync:** keeps `hidden_at` through an upsert and a full sync.
4. **Frontend units:**
   - The timeline layout (rows from counts and widths, jumping to a year, only visible months fetched).
   - Selection with Shift and the bulk actions.
   - The viewer's keys, swipes and crossing into the next month.
   - The map's point conversion and cluster thumbnails.
   - The picker: suggestions first, ticking, attaching, and "already added".
5. **Playwright** (stand-in Immich, nginx; 20,000 media rows inserted for the performance check):
   - The first screen shows within 2 s.
   - At most about 300 photo cells exist in the DOM at any time.
   - Jumping to the oldest year shows its photos.
   - Scrolling fetches months as they come into view.
   - Filters, including Videos and Hidden.
   - Selecting a range, then Hide.
   - The viewer's arrows across a month boundary, and a video playing.
   - The map with cluster thumbnails.
   - The picker from a place, a trip, a person and an itinerary item.
   - Phone and desktop screenshots.
