# Phase 3.1 — Map & Routes Implementation Plan

**Roadmap:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-completion-roadmap.md` → Milestone 3 → 3.1

**Goal:** every kind of item on the map gets a believable line and its details with little typing, and the map editor stops getting in the way.

- **Cruises:**
  - Look one up once, and it's kept.
  - A past cruise is found by its itinerary, drawn from its ports along shipping lanes, or proposed from your photos.
- **Flights:** any airport, and a flight number that fills in every leg and the date.
- **Road trips** follow roads.
- **Place search** answers as you type.
- **The map:**
  - Pins take the family's colours unless you pick one for an item.
  - It frames what the filters show.
  - The timeline keeps undated items.

**Decisions this plan relies on:**
- **§2 (2026-09-26):**
  - Cruises are essential: keep and harden CruiseMapper, with the real sailed route and ship details.
  - Flights: the world's airports plus flight-number lookup (AeroDataBox).
  - Geocoding: Photon.
  - Road trips follow roads.
- **§2 (2026-09-27), past cruises:** match a listed sailing by itinerary; otherwise ports with sea-routed legs; and cruises proposed from photos.

---

### Constraints found while planning

- **CruiseMapper lists only upcoming sailings.** A sailing's detail, fetched by its id, has the real track (`/map/cruise.json`) and the day-by-day ports (`/ships/cruise.json`).
  - "Reuse this itinerary" takes the listed sailing nearest in date, which for a past cruise is often a different route.
  - Matching by itinerary means fetching the details of candidate sailings: 2 requests each. The work is capped and every response is cached.
- **Sea routing:** `searoute-ts` 2.3 (MIT; its network is Eurostat's marnet, EUPL-1.2). Tried here:
  - Miami → Cozumel → Roatán → Costa Maya → Miami comes back as one line of about 3,100 km.
  - The first call takes about 0.5 s (loading the network), later calls about 75 ms, and the process uses about 120 MB.
  - The 20 km network gives finer coastal hops than the default 100 km. It's loaded on first use, in the backend only.
- **Ports:**
  - Today the table has **46 ports**, so most port searches fall through to Nominatim.
  - The new list merges:
    - The World Port Index (NGA Pub 150, public domain; via the MIT-licensed `tayljordan/ports` mirror, 5,410 records). It has Juneau, Skagway, Sitka, Costa Maya and Cozumel.
    - UN/LOCODE ports with coordinates (about 11,800). These cover Roatán and Santorini.
    - `searoute-ts`'s 1,610 ports.
  - Private cruise stops (Labadee, CocoCay, Half Moon Cay, Icy Strait Point) are in none of these. They come from CruiseMapper sailings, whose ports carry coordinates and are remembered in the table (`source = 'cruisemapper'`), and from place search. Anything else can be picked on the map.
- **Airports:** today **69**. OurAirports (public domain) has 9,054 airports with IATA codes.
- **Seeding** re-inserts reference data one row at a time on every start.
  - It moves to batched inserts, skipped when the bundled files haven't changed (a hash kept in the database).
  - Remembered CruiseMapper ports are kept.
- **Photon:** the public server is `photon.komoot.io` (fair use; can be self-hosted, so `PHOTON_URL`). Today as-you-type search goes to Nominatim, whose usage policy forbids autocomplete. Photon replaces Nominatim everywhere; results are cached.
- **Road routing:** any OSRM-compatible server, set with `ROUTING_URL`.
  - The default is `https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-car` (FOSSGIS's OSRM, used by openstreetmap.org, fair use).
  - It's asked only when a drive's stops change, and the result is stored.
  - On failure, the drive keeps straight lines and says so.
- **This sandbox can't reach** CruiseMapper, Photon, the OSRM servers or AeroDataBox. Every outside service gets a local stand-in for tests (like `fake-immich.ts`), and the real services are exercised on the preview image.
- **Photos at sea:**
  - Immich names a photo's city only within 25 km of a populated place (`reverseGeocodeMaxDistance`), and otherwise its country only from land borders (Natural Earth).
  - So a photo with a location but **no country** was taken at sea, or somewhere very remote. That's the signal for cruises found in photos.
  - A contract case checks it against the real Immich, and the stand-in behaves the same.
- **Pins are always explicit today.** The editor saves `color`, `icon`, `properties.pin` and `properties.path` for every item, so family, kind and cruise-line defaults never apply.
  - On the server, `NULL` already means "inherit". The editor saves only what the person changed.
  - A data migration clears values that equal the kind's old defaults (test data per §2; checked in `migrations.test.ts`).
- **Bugs found while planning:**
  - The editor:
    - Creating a visit twice on retry (no idempotency, and the editor doesn't switch to update after a create).
    - The first finished photo upload closes the editor.
    - Pick-on-map has no way out.
    - A pin click opens a popup *and* the detail modal.
  - Cruises:
    - `CRUISE_LOOKUP_ENABLED` is ignored by 4 of the 6 cruise routes.
    - `/api/lookup/cruise/diagnose` is open to any member, and follows redirects off the allowed hosts.
    - A sailing's port dates get the wrong year across New Year, and port times shift by the server's time zone.
  - The map:
    - Great-circle lines jump across the map at the antimeridian.
    - Dragging a cruise's waypoint replaces its sea track with arcs.
    - Re-looking-up an edited flight keeps the old line.
    - `PUT /api/settings` (family map settings) has no owner check and no validation.
  - Other:
    - `findPortDb` doesn't escape `%` and `_` in `LIKE`.
    - The flight lookup takes only the first leg.

### Data model (migration `0012_routes.sql`)

- **`lookup_cache`:** `(key TEXT PRIMARY KEY, value JSONB, fetched_at, expires_at)`.
  - One cache for CruiseMapper, Photon, OSRM and AeroDataBox responses, keyed by service and request.
  - Not backed up (`NOT_BACKED_UP`: it refills itself).
- **`ports`:**
  - Gains `country_code`, `locode`, `source` (`wpi`/`unlocode`/`searoute`/`cruisemapper`) and `aliases TEXT[]`.
  - Gains a trigram index on the name, for as-you-type search (`pg_trgm`).
  - Seeding replaces only the bundled sources.
- **`airports`:** gains `type` (large/medium/small) and `municipality`; a trigram index on name and city.
- **`reference_data`:** `(name, hash)`, so seeding is skipped when unchanged.
- **`visits.client_key UUID`,** unique per family: a create sent twice returns the first visit.
- **No new route columns.** A route stays the visit's LineString.
  - Where it came from and how long it is go in `properties.route = { source: 'cruisemapper' | 'sea' | 'road' | 'great-circle' | 'photos' | 'straight', distanceM }`.
  - A cruise's details go in `properties.cruise = { line, ship, shipUrl, shipImage, lineLogo, sailingId, sailingTitle, matchedBy: 'date' | 'itinerary' | null, shiftDays }`.
- **Data migration:** `color`/`icon` equal to the kind's default become `NULL`; `properties.pin`/`path` equal to the defaults are removed.

### Services (backend)

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| `lib/lookupCache.ts` | `cached(key, ttl, fn)`: read, or call and store. Failures aren't cached. |
| `services/seaRoute.ts` | `seaPath(points)` → `{ path, distanceM }` along shipping lanes (`searoute-ts`, 20 km network, loaded lazily). Points far from any lane snap to the nearest one. |
| `services/roadRoute.ts` | `roadPath(points)` → `{ path, distanceM }` from `ROUTING_URL` (OSRM `route/v1/driving`, `overview=full`, `geometries=geojson`), cached; up to 25 stops. |
| `services/photon.ts` | `searchPlaces(q, { near, limit })` and `geocode(q)`, from `PHOTON_URL`, cached a week. Replaces Nominatim; `NOMINATIM_*` are dropped. |
| `services/cruiseLookup.ts` | Hardened (below), plus `matchItinerary()`. |
| `lib/cruiseFromPhotos.ts` | `proposeCruise(scope, { from, to })` → ports, trail, and the photos it used. |
| `lib/geo.ts` | `greatCirclePath` keeps longitudes continuous across ±180 (MapLibre draws them correctly). |

**Cruise hardening:**
- **Caching:** ship pages for a day, sailing details for 90 days, lines and ships for a week.
- **Turned off:** every cruise route honours `CRUISE_LOOKUP_ENABLED` (409 "Cruise lookup is turned off").
- **Blocked:** a block is a 503 `{ blocked: true }` with the message "CruiseMapper isn't answering right now", and the UI offers **Try again**.
- **Diagnose:** admin only, and it doesn't follow redirects off the allowed hosts.
- **Dates:** port dates roll into the next year after December. Port times are the ship's local wall-clock times, stored as such and shown with `timeZone: "UTC"`.
- **Remembered ports:** every sailing's ports are added to `ports` (`source = 'cruisemapper'`).

**Itinerary matching** (`matchItinerary({ ship | shipUrl, line?, ports?, nights?, departurePort?, date })`):
1. Candidates:
   - The ship's listed sailings, then its sister ships' (same line; up to 12 ships, cached).
   - They're filtered by departure port and length (±1 night) when those are known.
2. The details of up to 10 candidates are fetched, cached.
3. Each is scored by its port sequence against the person's ports: longest common subsequence, where two ports match by name or within 30 km. With no ports, the score comes from departure port and length only.
4. The result is the best matches with their scores and `shiftDays` (the person's start date minus the sailing's).

**Cruises from photos** (`proposeCruise`):
1. Load the family's readable photos with a location between `from` and `to`, in time order.
2. **At sea:** a location but no country. **Ashore:** a country.
3. **Port calls:** for each day with photos ashore, take the port (from `ports`) nearest the day's photo closest to a port, within 40 km.
   - The same port on consecutive days is one call.
   - The first day's port is where the cruise begins and the last day's where it ends.
   - Days with only sea photos are sea days.
4. **The trail:**
   - It passes through the port calls and the sea photos in time order, thinned to one point per 2 hours.
   - It's routed along shipping lanes between points, so photos taken inland (an excursion) never pull it onto land.
5. **A proposal needs** 2 port calls, or 3 sea photos on 2 days. Otherwise the answer is "Not enough photos with a location to build this cruise".

### API

| Route | What it does |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/geo/search?q&near=lng,lat` | Photon. Same shape as today. |
| `GET /api/geo/ports?q`, `GET /api/geo/airports?q` | As-you-type over the new tables (trigram). |
| `POST /api/routes/sea` `{ points }` | `{ path, distanceM }` |
| `POST /api/routes/road` `{ points }` | `{ path, distanceM }`, or 503 with the reason (the editor keeps straight lines). |
| `POST /api/lookup/cruise/match` `{ ship?, shipUrl?, line?, ports?, date, nights? }` | `{ matches: [{ sailing, ports, path, score, shiftDays }] }` |
| `POST /api/cruises/from-photos` `{ from, to }` | `{ ports, path, photoIds, seaPhotos }`, or 422 with why. Family data: an isolation case. |
| `POST /api/lookup/flight` | Now every leg of a flight number, with its date. 400 for a date AeroDataBox can't answer, with its window. |
| `POST /api/visits` | Accepts `clientKey`. A repeat returns the existing visit (200). |
| `PUT /api/settings` | Owner or co-owner only, validated with zod. |
| Suggestions | A new kind, `new-cruise:<from>:<to>:<lat>,<lng>`. Applying it creates the trip and a cruise from the proposal, and attaches the photos. |

### Frontend

- **Visit editor:**
  - **Staying open:**
    - Photos added to a saved item refresh its photo list without closing the editor.
    - After the first save the editor switches to update, and sends a `clientKey`.
    - Escape and **Cancel** end pick-on-map (the Promise resolves `null`).
  - **Cruise form:**
    - Line, ship and sail date, then **Find on CruiseMapper**.
    - When the date is past and nothing is listed then, it says so and offers three ways in:
      1. **Find the same itinerary**, from your ports (or departure port and length): pick a match, and its track is shifted to your dates.
      2. **Build from ports:** ports from the world list, one per day, joined along shipping lanes.
      3. **Build from my photos:** the start and end dates, then the proposal, editable.
    - It keeps "reuse this itinerary" and stores the cruise's details.
    - Adding, moving or removing a port re-routes along shipping lanes, unless the track came from CruiseMapper and the ports are unchanged.
  - **Flight form:**
    - An airport picker (as-you-type, the new list) for each leg.
    - A flight number plus date fills every leg and sets the item's date.
    - Re-looking-up clears the old line.
  - **Drive form:** the route follows roads, re-routed (debounced) when stops change. The distance is shown and saved.
- **Map:**
  - A pin click opens one detail view (no popup); a stack still opens its list.
  - Dragging a waypoint re-routes by the item's route source.
  - The map re-frames to the visible items when the filters change (not when data refreshes, and not while an item is open).
- **Timeline:**
  - Undated items stay on the map, with a "No date (n)" chip that hides or shows them.
  - The axis gets year and month ticks, and a strip showing how many items fall on each part of it.
- **Styling:**
  - The Appearance tab shows the inherited colour and icon with **Use default**. Only changed values are saved.
  - The legend comes from `resolveItemStyle` with the family's settings (by kind, and cruise lines on the map).
  - Pins and paths resolve in one order: item → theme → cruise line → kind → family → base.
  - **Map appearance** moves to a kit dialog: defaults by kind and by cruise line, and themes editable (name, colour, icon, line), for owners.
  - Shared pages use the family's settings too.
- **Suggestions:** "Looks like a cruise: Miami → Cozumel → Roatán, 3–10 March 2019 (86 photos). Create it?"

### Tests (TDD, in order)

1. **Migration:**
   - The new tables and columns.
   - A repeated `clientKey`.
   - The style data migration on old-shape data (rolled back).
   - `BACKUP_TABLES` coverage (`lookup_cache` not backed up).
2. **Reference data:**
   - The build script's merge (dedupe by name and country within 5 km).
   - Seeding: batched, skipped when unchanged, remembered ports kept.
   - Search: trigram ranking, `%` and `_` escaped.
3. **Services, each with a local stand-in server** (`test/fake-web.ts`: CruiseMapper pages and JSON, Photon, OSRM, AeroDataBox):
   - `seaPath` stays off land for known pairs (checked against a few coastal points).
   - `roadPath`, including failure.
   - Photon mapping and caching.
   - Cruise caching, blocked and turned-off cases, and the year rollover.
   - `matchItinerary` scoring.
   - Flight multi-leg and date checks.
4. **`proposeCruise`:** a seeded photo set (embarkation, 2 ports with an inland excursion, sea days) gives the right calls and a trail that doesn't cross land. Plus the too-few-photos case.
5. **Routes and isolation:**
   - Every new route has a case in `tenant-isolation.test.ts` (reference and lookup routes stay `EXEMPT`; `from-photos` is family data).
   - The `new-cruise` suggestion (apply and dismiss).
6. **Contract (Immich):** a photo with a location mid-ocean has no city and no country. The stand-in's `reverseGeocode` does the same.
7. **Frontend units:**
   - The editor: stays open on upload; one visit on retry; pick cancel.
   - The cruise form's three past-cruise paths; the flight form's legs and date.
   - Drive re-routing (debounced).
   - The Appearance tab's inherit and override.
   - The legend, the map re-fit on filter change, and the timeline's undated chip.
8. **Playwright** (stand-ins for the outside services, nginx, desktop and phone):
   - Add a past cruise all three ways.
   - A road trip that follows roads.
   - A flight by number with two legs.
   - As-you-type place search.
   - Family pin colours applying to existing items.
   - Filters re-framing the map.
   - Undated items on the timeline.
   - A screenshot of each route for a visual check that sea lines stay on water.
