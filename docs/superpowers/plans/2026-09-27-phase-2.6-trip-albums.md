# Phase 2.6 — Trip ↔ Immich Album Sync Implementation Plan

**Roadmap:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-completion-roadmap.md` → Milestone 2 → 2.6 (design in §3.6)

**Goal:** every trip is also an album in Immich, in each family's own account, and the two stay the same.
- Putting a photo in a trip in Werejugo puts it in the album straight away. Taking it out, renaming the trip or deleting the trip follow.
- Adding photos to the album in Immich's own app or web page puts them in the trip in Werejugo, within minutes (or at once with **Refresh from Immich**). Removing them from the album takes them out of the trip.

**Decisions this plan relies on:**
- **§2 (2026-09-27):** Werejugo also sees what's done in Immich directly (owner's answer). Families can use Immich's app with their own password (2.1).
- **§3.3:** another family's photos appear only in the shared trip in Werejugo. Immich-level sharing isn't needed; each family's album holds its own photos of the trip.

---

### Constraints found while planning

- **Immich 3's album response has no asset list.** An album's photos come from the structured search with `albumIds`. `getAllAlbums` gives each album's `updatedAt` and `assetCount`, which is how the sync notices a change in Immich without reading every album. The contract test checks what the real Immich changes when assets are added or removed. The nightly full sync reads every album anyway.
- **A photo is in at most one trip in Werejugo** (`media.trip_id`), but can be in several albums in Immich. A photo added in Immich to a second trip's album moves to that trip (the most recent change wins), and leaves the first trip's album at the next pass.
- **`trip_id` is written in many places** (photo details, bulk actions, the picker, suggestions, a place's trip, the upload hand-off, deleting a trip). Rather than hooking each one, a **database trigger** marks the family's album for that trip as changed. The routes also ask for an album sync straight away, so it happens within seconds. A missed call is caught by the next 5-minute sync.
- **Which side changed:** the sync keeps each album's contents as of the last pass (the **base**). Then:
  - in Werejugo but not in the base → added in Werejugo → add to the album;
  - in the base but not in Werejugo → removed in Werejugo → remove from the album;
  - in the album but not in the base → added in Immich → put the photo in the trip;
  - in the base but not in the album → removed in Immich → take the photo out of the trip, if it's still in it.

  A photo changed on both sides since the last pass follows Werejugo.
- **An album deleted in Immich** is made again rather than emptying the trip: deleting an album there shouldn't take hundreds of photos out of a trip. It's logged. Removing photos from the album is how to take them out in Immich.
- **Album names follow the trip.** Renaming the album in Immich is undone at the next pass (logged in the README).
- **Hidden photos** stay in the album. Hiding is Werejugo's own flag, and Immich is untouched.
- **A family that leaves a shared trip** keeps its album in Immich but it's no longer synced (its photos keep their trip, as 1.5 decided, so they return if the family is invited back, and so does the syncing).

### Data model (migration `0009_trip_albums.sql`)

- **`trip_albums`:** `(family_id, trip_id)` unique; `trip_id` → trips `ON DELETE SET NULL`, so a deleted trip's album can still be deleted in Immich.
  - `immich_album_id`, `name`, `synced_assets UUID[]` (the base), `immich_updated_at`, `immich_asset_count`.
  - `dirty` (Werejugo changed something), `synced_at`, `last_error`.
- **Triggers:**
  - `media` after insert or update of `trip_id`: mark the family's album for the old and the new trip dirty (inserting the new trip's row if needed).
  - `trips` after update of `name`: mark its albums dirty.
  - `trip_members` after insert: the joining family's album for the trip is created at the next pass (no trigger needed: the pass lists the trips each family is on).

### Adapter and contract additions

- `immich.listAlbums(conn)` (owned albums).
- `createAlbum(conn, name, description, assetIds)`, `updateAlbum(conn, id, name)`, `deleteAlbum(conn, id)`.
- `addToAlbum(conn, id, assetIds)`, `removeFromAlbum(conn, id, assetIds)` (batches of 500).
- Searching assets by album: `filter.albumIds.any`.
- **Contract tests** (stand-in and real Immich):
  - Create an album with a photo; list it; search it by album.
  - Add and remove a photo; rename; `updatedAt` and `assetCount` after each change.
  - Delete the album; the photo is still there.
- **Stand-in:** albums, their assets, search by album.

### Sync (`lib/immich/albums.ts`)

`syncAlbums(familyId, { full })` runs after the library and faces in each family sync, and on its own when a route asks for it:

1. The family's trips: those it hosts or is a member of. Each gets a row and an album, even when empty, so photos can be put in a trip from Immich.
2. `listAlbums` once. An album is reconciled when its row is dirty, has no album yet, or its album's `updatedAt`/`assetCount` changed, or on the full (nightly) pass.
3. **Reconcile:**
   - W = the family's photos in the trip; I = the album's assets (search by album, not trashed); B = the base.
   - Add W−B to the album, remove B−W from it.
   - I−B: put those photos in the trip, if Werejugo has them. Skip the ones it doesn't have yet; they'll be there next time.
   - B−I: take those photos out of the trip, if they're still in it.
   - Rename the album if the trip's name changed. The new base is what the album now holds.
4. **Rows whose trip was deleted:** delete the album in Immich, then the row.
5. Errors are stored per album (`last_error`), and the rest carry on.

**When it runs:** after each library sync (every 5 minutes, nightly in full), on **Refresh from Immich**, and straight away (a pg-boss job per family, one at a time) after anything in Werejugo changes a photo's trip, a trip's name, or deletes a trip.

### Frontend

- **A trip's Photos section:** "Also an album in your Immich: *Italy 2024*". The line shows when the album was last synced, or its error.
- **README:** how the albums work, what Immich-side changes do, and that the album's name follows the trip.

### Tests (TDD, in order)

1. **Migration:** the table; the triggers mark rows dirty for a photo moved between trips and a trip renamed; a deleted trip leaves its row with no trip.
2. **Album sync (stand-in):**
   - An album is made for each trip the family is on, with the trip's photos.
   - Werejugo → Immich: moving a photo between trips moves it between albums; renaming a trip renames its album; deleting a trip deletes its album and keeps the photos.
   - Immich → Werejugo: a photo added to the album joins the trip; removed from the album, it leaves the trip.
   - Both sides: a photo moved in Werejugo and added to another album in Immich in the same interval follows Werejugo. Adding in Immich to a second album moves the photo.
   - An album deleted in Immich is made again with the trip's photos.
   - A shared trip: each family's album holds only its own photos. A family that left isn't synced.
   - Unchanged albums aren't read again (the calls are counted).
3. **Routes:** the album route; the kicks (moving a photo through `PATCH /api/media/:id` updates the album by the time the job settles); isolation.
4. **Frontend units:** the trip's album line (synced, error, none).
5. **Playwright** (stand-in, nginx):
   - Move photos into a trip in Werejugo, and they're in its album in the stand-in.
   - Add one to the album through the stand-in's API, press **Refresh from Immich**, and it's in the trip.
   - Rename the trip, and the album follows.
   - Phone and desktop.
